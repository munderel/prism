# Mohamed's App Review — March 25, 2026

## Transcript Summary

### Navigation & Cognitive Load
- Wants a **toggle to hide the left sidebar** so it doesn't distract from the main content
- Wants different views — one **super simple "My Stuff" view** that says "work on this right now"
- CRO mindset: **every additional link, element, feature is a distraction** that decreases the likelihood you get the thing done
- Labels like "Goal Stack", "React Task", "Maintenance" are **hard to understand without extra context**

### Goal Stack
- **Likes the goal stack a lot** — "I like this. This is 100 cool"
- Could be **overwhelming** — wants ability to break it down by High Hard Goal
- Wants to **toggle one level at a time** (HHG, yearly, monthly, weekly)
- KPIs are good — can add them and track progress toward goals
- For weekly/monthly/yearly goals: wants a **progress bar showing time urgency** — "you have 4 weeks left" / "2 weeks left"
- Wants a **toggle to see stuff due today** or most recent items
- Monthly goals are confusing — "is it 10 months from now? 1 month from now? 3 months from now? I don't know"

### Aims
- Aims are based on Flow Research Collective material
- You toggle aims on/off, then they show up as things to schedule in the calendar
- Wants **habit progression/ramp-up feature** — not everyone can jump to 90 min deep work immediately
- Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3, Phase 4 — progressive habit building
- References Atomic Habits approach: start with 5 minutes, build to 15, etc.
- "They're gonna fail unless they have like these pretty habits and they can easily attach it"
- Should have a **logical ramp-up** — "you want to ramp up starting this habit"
- Some aims need explanation — users "might not even understand what active recovery even is"

### Calendar & Scheduling
- When aims are toggled on, they should **show up in Google Calendar** for scheduling
- Calendar events should allow **grouping with other people** — so others can join the same activity
- Example: group flow sessions, team exercise
- Calendar should be the **single source of truth** — "if it's not in their calendar, they didn't do it"
- **No auto-recurring task completion** — users must manually manage input, can't just leave recurring items
- This forces them to actually use the calendar and makes progress tracking more accurate

### Reviews
- Review cadences should be **pre-made but adjustable** based on schedules
- Reviews should be **grouped as a team** — "discuss them as a team, like how were your tasks"
- Weekly reviews can stay individual
- Can assess if someone is following through based on their calendar activity

### Leaderboard
- Important feature — **third priority** after Goal Stack and Calendar
- Leaderboard should be tied to reviews and activities

### Processes
- Three types: maintenance, react, and improve (from Process Driven methodology)
- Important for **delegation** — assign processes to people
- Shows cadence of recurring business tasks
- Links to SOPs (standard operating procedures)
- If there are no processes, it doesn't affect anything — it's a feature, not a necessity
- Wants it there for future delegation needs

### Training/Books
- **Never tested this feature** — "I never touched this, it's a new feature"
- Recommends **removing/hiding it for now** — save effort for core features
- "The more stuff you add, it's going to be 100 times harder"

### Strategic Priorities (Mohamed's recommended order)
1. **Goal Stack** — most important
2. **Calendar** — most important
3. **Leaderboard** — third
4. Everything else is secondary

### Core Design Philosophy
- "The more useful it is, the more they use it"
- Personal stuff goes in Google Calendar, team/business stuff in the app
- Design for your own use first — that translates to other people
- Every feature should reduce friction, not add it
